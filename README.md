## 👋 About me

I’m **Alexis**, a **SysOps Engineer and IT Teacher** based in Lausanne, Switzerland. Systems and infrastructure are my foundation; automation, custom development, and a growing interest in AI expand what I can build.

I enjoy solving practical IT challenges and moving projects forward — from technical assessment and infrastructure design to development, deployment, hosting, and ongoing improvement. I also build personal and professional software projects to turn repetitive work and disconnected tools into focused, maintainable solutions.

- 🧭 **IT consulting** — infrastructure, operations, and technical decisions
- 🧩 **Custom development** — full-stack, mobile, and automation solutions
- 🚀 **Hosting & deployment** — giving applications and services a reliable home
- 🎓 **IT education** — making computer science clear, practical, and useful
- 🌱 Currently learning **Portuguese**

> Your IT challenges. Practical solutions.
